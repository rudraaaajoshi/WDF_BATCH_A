Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I register on the StudentHub Portal?

Open the Registration page, fill in all the required details, and click the Register button.

2. How can I log in to my account?

Go to the Login page, enter your Student ID and Password, then click the Login button.

3. How do I check my attendance?

Click on the Attendance page from the navigation menu to view your attendance record.

4. Where can I see my results?

Open the Results page to view your grades, SGPA, and CGPA.

5. How can I update my profile?

Visit the Profile page to view your personal and academic information.

6. Who should I contact if I face any issue?

Visit the Contact page and fill out the contact form or call the college support number.

Necessary Tips

  • Keep your login credentials secure.
  • Check the portal regularly for notices and updates.
  • Maintain at least 75% attendance.
  • Contact the college office if you forget your password.
